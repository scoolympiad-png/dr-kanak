# Dr Kanak digital footprint — clinical SEO growth programme
Private strategy and editorial working document. Date: 29 October 2026.
Website: https://drkanak.com | Clinical base: Mamta Super Speciality Hospital, Mowa, Raipur, Chhattisgarh.

## North-star positioning
Make Dr Kanak's website a credible, parent-centred paediatric information and appointment gateway. The geographic footprint has THREE different meanings:
- **Raipur / Chhattisgarh:** authentic clinic discovery and appointment interest. Practice at the verified Mamta location. Prioritise relevant queries for nearby families, but do not invent city offices or availability.
- **India:** practical family education and Hindi+English material tailored to the Indian care context and official schedule. Search visibility is not the same as nationwide physical service.
- **Worldwide:** clinically grounded, accessible education useful to parents globally. Clearly distinguish Indian national guidance from US/WHO recommendations and avoid implying overseas clinical services.

Clinical positioning verification: https://mamtasuperspecialityhospital.com/doctors/kanak-ramnani/
Verified in public source: MBBS; DNB Pediatrics Gold Medalist; fellowship training in Pediatric Epilepsy & Neurology, Associate Professor; care related to newborns, child development, vaccination, neurology and paediatric health. Do not use "general practitioner", "autism specialist", "autism cure" or "best paediatrician" without specific independent substantiation.

## Primary content clusters and intent
1. **Autism and developmental understanding (authority priority):** /autism/, /autism/early-signs/, /autism/sensory-needs/, /autism/communication-support/, /autism/assessment/; next parent school-and-play, parent FAQ, speech-language milestones, hearing considerations, school adjustments and support after diagnosis. Every page answers a distinct parental question, not the same keywords in slightly different copy.
2. **Vaccination and prevention (high-intent care):** /vaccination/, /vaccination/schedule/, /vaccination/catch-up/, /vaccination/after-care/, /vaccination/reminders/; next record keeping, first-year preparation, premature infants after clinical review, travel and adolescent HPV. No automated individual dosing from public web content.
3. **General paediatrics and newborn care:** common illnesses, cough/fever signs, when to seek urgent help, nutrition, growth, sleep, common skin concerns, newborn follow-up, adolescent wellbeing. Support parents; no drug dosage prescribing or self-diagnosis.
4. **Paediatric neurological concerns:** explaining seizure first-aid and what to do at visits, epilepsy questions, developmental and neurological referrals. Require high-priority clinician review; no unsupported treatment promises.
5. **Age-based parenting:** 0-1, toddlers, preschool, primary school, adolescence; useful checklists, parent handouts, original short videos.

## 30-day foundational sprint (baseline before claim)
1. Audit actual public deployment: domain/redirect, robots, sitemap, canonicals, indexability, responsive navigation, images, form/phone CTA, Open Graph, errors and performance. Do not infer production status from GitHub build alone.
2. Obtain authenticated Google Search Console and GA4 baseline for this domain: last 28 days clicks, impressions, indexed URLs, branded vs nonbranded queries, geo/device breakdown, top pages and CTR. No invented numbers.
3. Verify hospital doctor profile, exact public doctor name, address, phone, credentials and back-link. Coordinate one official referral link from Mamta profile to drkanak.com if hospital approves. Avoid cross-domain duplication and competing pages with the same title.
4. Google Business Profile: check existing practitioner profile and eligibility before creating anything. Follow official guidance for individually reachable physicians at an accurately represented location. Do not create one profile per service, keyword or city.
5. Improve /autism/ cluster with one substantial new guide at a time, source links and relevant hub/child backlinks; no thin place pages.
6. Parent-focused FAQ in English and verified Hindi where natural; clinical edit/approval before displaying "reviewed by" claims.
7. Draft an authentic video plan with Dr Kanak explaining child development, vaccination record planning and general health questions. Use official filming rights and consent; do not create patient testimonials or fake claims.

## Measured scorecard (targets are activity targets until baseline exists)
- Coverage: all priority routes internally linked and present in correct sitemap; indexation only confirmed through GSC.
- Content: 2 clinically-reviewed, distinct new guides per week when reviewer capacity permits; no mass-produced thin pages.
- Video: 2–4 short original parent-education videos monthly after doctor approves.
- Local signals: maintain one accurate primary practitioner/hospital identity and regular authentic authorised updates.
- Conversion quality: user clicks to directions, telephone, appointment enquiries; actual clinic appointments only when hospital/CRM confirms them. No PHI to marketing analytics.
- Weekly review: crawl errors and funnel breakage; monthly: 28-day GSC comparisons for impressions, clicks, CTR and non-branded terms.
- Outcome targets for rankings and traffic to be set only AFTER real GSC baseline. No guarantees of #1 or a falsely asserted local competition score.
