# Approved Dr Kanak K identity — 28 October 2026

Approved by the project owner for deployment across drkanak.com.

## Brand mark

The distinctive **K** is composed of a parent-and-child embrace, hopeful star and leaf-like shapes. It uses teal, deep blue, pink, mint and gold. This brand replaces the earlier heart-only emblem; do not reintroduce that symbol as the site or downloadable-material logo.

- `/images/dr-kanak-logo-k.png`: transparent K logo for the website header, footer and future section branding.
- `/images/dr-kanak-logo.svg`: compatibility version of the approved K asset.
- `/favicon.ico`, `/favicon-32.png`, `/favicon-48.png`, `/favicon.svg`: favicon assets.

- `/downloads/dr-kanak-family-vaccination-companion.pdf`: header uses K logo; schedule and clinical content unchanged.

## Usage principles

- Keep a white background behind the K at small sizes when on a teal navigation surface.
- Preserve original proportions and generous breathing space. Never stretch, rotate or recolour individual shapes independently.
- Only use the approved professional title **Paediatrician** in branded lockups unless additional qualifications are verified directly with the hospital.
- Dr Kanak's genuine photography and Mamta's own hospital logo are separate assets; do not overwrite hospital branding.
- Future downloadable guides, vaccination PDFs, social cards and resource covers should adopt the K logo as their primary physician-brand mark.
- For accessibility, the named website brand link carries the screen-reader label; the decorative K icon image has empty alt text.

The original visual direction is the image-generation brand board approved during this project. The production transparent K asset was extracted from that approved concept and optimised for actual web usage. The symbol is provided as PNG for pixel-perfect rendition; the SVG compatibility wrapper embeds that PNG.
