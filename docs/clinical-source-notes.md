# Parent resource content

Ten original English guides are in `parent-guides.json`. Each contains 343 to 373 English words including its title, summary, sections and three FAQs. Hindi titles and summaries are supplied, not full Hindi articles. Related guide slugs have been validated.

The sources are primary IAP, WHO, CDC, AAP HealthyChildren and NHS resources. Dr Dina, Dr Golly and Dr Greene articles have not been copied or used as clinical source text. There are 26 distinct source URLs. Source links are supplied per guide for the website renderer.

The copy does not claim that Dr Kanak has written or medically reviewed it. Do not add a medical review badge or doctor attribution unless that review has actually happened. These are general education guides, not a personalised care plan. No medicine doses, automatic diagnoses or promises of clinical service availability are included.

## Clinical editing notes

General guides cite their original IAP, WHO, AAP HealthyChildren, CDC and NHS sources in `lib/parent-guides.json`. The source links render on each article. Do not reproduce source PDFs or articles in full.

- Fever guidance uses urgent assessment for a baby younger than three months with a temperature of 38°C or higher.
- Seizure first aid uses emergency assessment for a first seizure, an episode reaching five minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, breathing difficulty or serious injury. It does not give rescue medicine doses. UK emergency numbers from source pages have been omitted; the separate Mamta emergency route supplies verified Indian hospital contacts.
- Newborn warning signs include a baby who is difficult to wake or not feeding, blue colouring, green vomit or breathing problems. No home-treatment substitute is provided.
- Milestone checklists are distinguished from diagnostic assessment. Growth is described as a pattern over time rather than a single percentile target.
- No dose table, DOB calculator, vaccine eligibility rule or medicine dosing is deployed. The supplied blank hospital card has ambiguous abbreviations and does not by itself establish current clinical rules.
- IAP's schedule titled 2025 was published in April 2026. HPV guidance and the 2026 public programme are described separately. Review the current official schedule and product guidance before making a personalised plan.
