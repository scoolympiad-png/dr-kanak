## Sources verified
- CDC: https://www.cdc.gov/autism/diagnosis/index.html (reviewed October 2026): monitoring, screening vs diagnostic evaluation; no single medical diagnostic test.
- CDC screening for professionals: https://www.cdc.gov/autism/hcp/diagnosis/screening.html (reviewed October 2026): AAP US screening intervals 18 and 24 months; not presented as Indian medical guideline.
- IAP Action Plan 2026: https://www.iapindia.org/iap-action-plan-2026/ (autism recognition, developmental support and referral topics).
- WHO: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/autism-spectrum-disorders
