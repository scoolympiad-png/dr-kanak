## Three uploaded PDFs — what they support and limits

1. **Adobe Scan Oct 24, 2026.pdf** — four photographed pages of Mamta vaccination administration register. Contains identifying child/adult names, phone numbers, ages, timestamps, brand/batch/expiry details, staff names and notes. **Confidential clinical information.** Do not commit scans, OCR extracts, identifiable records, thumbnails or rows to public repo, CDN, CMS, analytics, search, or ChatGPT website content. At most, derive generic education principles: complete and accurate vaccination recordkeeping and clinician reconciliation with past vaccination dates.
2. **Adobe Scan 24 Oct 2026 (1).pdf** — 15 cropped book pages. Topics include older-children/hemodialysis hepatitis B antibody follow-up, premature infant hepatitis B context, post-exposure prophylaxis, maternal pertussis / Tdap, tetanus wound care, high-risk pneumococcal questions, varicella, hepatitis A and HPV. The source book title, edition and publication date cannot be reliably identified from the excerpts alone, and some page-level recommendations may be historical. Do not publish exact doses, intervals or treatment rules from the extracts as current medical guidance; use as themes for clinical review and verify against current jurisdiction-specific recommendations.
3. **Adobe Scan 24 Oct 2026.pdf** — three cropped book pages on vaccine precautions/contraindications and recordkeeping, polio National Immunization Days/campaigns, and IAP polio guidance. Historical programmatic wording is unsuitable as an unverified current Indian schedule. Use as parent-education themes only.

## Independently verifiable clinical support

### ADHD
- CDC: ADHD in the classroom — https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/treatment/classroom.html
- American Academy of Pediatrics: clinical ADHD guideline — https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/144/4/e20192528/81572/Clinical-Practice-Guideline-for-the-Diagnosis
- Indian Academy of Pediatrics national consensus on ADHD — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28368271/
- Systematic review on ADHD with intellectual giftedness — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37929569/
- Evidence supports organisational training, parent–teacher communication, task segmentation and immediate positive feedback as supportive strategies. These activities are not a substitute for diagnosis, appropriate clinical evaluation or individual multimodal treatment.

### Vaccination
- MoHFW, India: Routine Immunization Manual for Health Workers (2024) — https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Routine%20Immunization%20Manual%20For%20Health%20Workers.pdf
- IAP immunisation guidance — https://acvip.org/professional/
- CDC: vaccination contraindications and precautions — https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-best-practices/contraindications-precautions.html
- Parent-facing content discusses what to bring, relevant risks, and questions to ask; it does not generate due dates or declare a vaccine contraindicated for a specific patient.

## Patient safety and branding
- **No patient-identifiable material** from any uploaded PDF should ever be public.
- Only the two user-supplied ADHD graphics are published. **The graphics themselves label Dr Kanak “Pediatrician & Pediatric Neurologist.”** The official Mamta page currently lists “Consultant Pediatrician,” DNB Pediatrics and fellowship training in Pediatric Epilepsy & Neurology. Confirm the exact professional designation with the hospital before broader distribution; the prose uses “Dr Kanak” or “Paediatrician,” not an unverified board credential.
- The upload is user-provided artwork. Preserve its text in the image and provide original explanations on the webpage rather than transcribing graphic text verbatim.
- Continue normal clinical review of parent materials, especially treatment, medication or vaccine information.
